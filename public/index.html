The Process

Painted from the moment, not the merchandise.

Eric Samuel Timm has spent more than 20 years bringing art and storytelling to life in front of over a million people across 39 countries. His love of collecting sports cards as a kid has come full circle into original artwork capturing the players, moments, and memories that made the game.

Each portrait begins the same way — pulling a single frame from the exact moment an athlete became a legend, then rebuilding it by hand in acrylic and ink. No filters, no print runs pretending to be originals.

“I see art as three fold: a mirror that reflects your memories and moments, a window that shows you possibility, and a door that invites my viewers toward greatness despite the failures and flaws these icons all had.”

— Eric Samuel Timm

Up close: acrylic, ink, and raw canvas grain — every mark is by hand.
