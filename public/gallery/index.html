The Process

Painted from the moment, not the merchandise.

Each portrait begins the same way — pulling a single frame from the exact moment an athlete became a legend, then rebuilding it by hand in acrylic and ink. No filters, no print runs pretending to be originals.

The result sits somewhere between a vintage trading card and a fine art portrait: bold enough for a man cave, restrained enough for a formal room. Commissions are taken in limited batches so every canvas gets full attention.

— Eric Samuel Timm
